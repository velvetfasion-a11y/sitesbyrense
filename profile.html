FAQ

How do I update my website?

Send a message in your chat with Rensé and describe the changes you'd like.

How do I pay my subscription?

Go to Settings → Subscription. Your plan and amount are set by Rensé — checkout is handled securely via Stripe.

How do I cancel?

Go to Settings → Contact & Support to cancel. Your access continues until the end of your billing period.
