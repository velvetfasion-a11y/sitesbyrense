Help

How do I update my website?

Send a message in your chat with Rensé and describe the changes you'd like.

How do I pay my subscription?

You'll receive a payment link via email before your renewal date. You can also pay in advance under Settings → Subscription.

How do I cancel?

You can cancel anytime under Settings → Subscription. Your access continues until the end of your billing period.
